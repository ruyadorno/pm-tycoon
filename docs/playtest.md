# Playtest 2: the brief and `playtest.json` v2

## The brief

Play **thirty to forty minutes, cold**, on the iPhone 16: a new game, no seed typed, no `?config=`, no help beyond what
the game shows. The run to watch is level 1 → level 3 (the Docks and the ship) and, if it goes well, level 5 and the
Golden Release. The policy timeline (the 10-seed sim gate's medians with the card deck on, docs/gate/latest.md) puts L2
at ~10.7 min, L3 at ~16.5, L4 at ~20.6 and L5 at ~25.3 — game time; the pauses on cards come on top, and a cold human
is expected later.

Build 3 adds the **cards** (README "Cards"): the first opens at 3:20 of game time (Day 4), then one every 60–100 s of
game time at L1, a little less often at higher levels. **Every card pauses the game** until you choose — no timer, no
Later — and prints each option's effects under it; job offers are `JOB BOARD` cards with **Accept** / **Decline**. The
effects chip in the top bar (and **Effects** in the pause menu) lists what is running. The Golden Release keeps its
gold card, which does not pause the game, and its **Later**. `playtest.json` stays v2 in this build: it does not record
the cards, and `pausedS` includes the time a card was up.

At **30:00 of game time** (and never before 25 real minutes on screen, so at 2× it waits for the wall clock) a ticker
line and a toast offer the run summary. Open it then if you are done, or keep playing and open it whenever you stop:
tap ❚❚ to pause, ❚❚ again for the pause menu → **End run — show summary**. The card shows the run's numbers and the
**fun bar 2** below; **Download playtest.json** saves the profile and **Copy summary** copies the card as text. Send
either with your notes.

## Where to find it

`playtest.json` is the profile of one run: what happened, when, and how the phone coped. The recorder is
`src/metrics/playtest.ts` (plan.md §12.9, plan-build2 §8.7 task 1); `tests/metrics/playtest-schema.test.ts` pins every
field and type, and `tests/playtest/seed-7.json` is a real example written by `vlr shots` from a 35-minute automated run
(seed 7, the competent policy: the first 6 minutes in real time at 4×, then fast-forwarded, so its `elapsedS` and `fps`
cover only the real-time part). Nothing is sent anywhere: the file stays on the device until you download or copy it.

## Units

- Every field ending in `S` is **sim seconds** (ticks ÷ 10), except `elapsedS` and `pausedS`, which are **wall-clock
  seconds**. At 1× speed one sim second is one real second; at 2× it is half of one.
- One **in-game minute** = one **in-game day** = 60 sim seconds (spec §2): payday, satisfaction drift, the daily ticker
  line and the applicant pools' refresh happen on that boundary. A thirty-minute session at 1× is about thirty days.
- `null` means "not reached" (no truck bought yet, level 3 not reached, no Victory, …), never zero.
- Money is in game dollars, integers.

## The fun bar 2 (plan-build2 §9)

The card shows six checks from the same object: ✓ pass, ✗ fail, `?` **you judge** (the data part holds, the rest is
how it felt — only you can say), `–` not yet (the window is still open).

1. **Hiring was a choice** — at least one hire where you did not pick the cheapest applicant (`hires[].cheapest` is
   `false`): reads `?` with how many, because the real question is whether you can say _why_ you picked them (the pips
   were readable, the salaries felt like a trade-off). ✗ when every pick was the cheapest; `–` before the first hire.
2. **A quest done by 15:00** — `quests.firstCompletedS ≤ 900`. Did you want the next one?
3. **Level 3 between 15:00 and 22:00** — `900 ≤ level3AtS ≤ 1320`. Did the Docks and the ship read as a new beat?
4. **Kept playing past 25:00** — `elapsedS > 1500` at the moment you first opened the summary card (**real** minutes on
   screen, paused time included, not the game clock: at 2× the game clock runs ahead; the recorder keeps that moment,
   so a peek at 20:00 counts against it). The offer never comes before 25 real minutes, so opening it when offered
   passes.
5. **Every lost life explained** — every `livesLost[]` entry has a `counter`, a `sorter` or `clearedByPlayer: true`; an
   overseas loss (`hub` set) also says "the ship has sailed" / "the plane has left" on its card. Reads `?` when the data
   explains every loss (did the lost-life card make sense?), ✓ when none was lost, ✗ with the count of unexplained ones.
6. **(stretch) Level 5 or Victory by 40:00** — `level5AtS ≤ 2400` or `victoryAtS ≤ 2400`. A ✗ here is not a failure
   of the build, just a slower run.

The sim gate predicts 2, 3 and 5 for a competent player (docs/gate/latest.md, seeds 1–10 medians with the card deck on:
6 quests offered and 3 completed by 30 min, L3 16.5 min, L5 25.3 min, lives 0); 1, 4 and 6 only your run can answer.

## Reading a profile

- **Pacing**: compare `level2AtS` … `level5AtS`, `victoryAtS` and `quests.firstCompletedS` with the gate medians above. A
  late level with a flat `moneyPerMin` means the fleet stayed small: check `vehiclesBought`, `docksBought` and
  `overseas.cleared` (overseas parcels pay ×1.5 from L3).
- **People**: `hires` shows who you took and what you paid (`cheapest` false = you paid for pips); `fired`, `adsPosted`
  and `payroll.short` / `walkouts` show whether the crew felt manageable. `sorter.wrong` vs `sorter.decisions` is how much
  you could trust the sorter; `sorter.clearedMalware` is how often its `looks clean` tag was wrong.
- **Quests**: `quests.byTemplate` shows which jobs you took and finished; many `declined` means the offers did not
  appeal, many `failed` that the deadlines felt unfair.
- **Decisions**: `holdBayEvents` vs `malware.tossedClean` and `malware.cleared` shows how you treated flagged parcels.
- **Phone performance**: `fps.p50 < 50` or `fps.p5 < 30` triggers the cut order of plan.md §12.8 (`src/render/quality.ts`).
  `device.zoom` should be 1 on the iPhone 16 (393 CSS px).
- **Not a cold run**: `buildStamp` ending in `cfg:spicy` / `cfg:tycoon`, or a `seed` you typed, means a debug session.

Send the file (or the copied summary) with your notes; together with the seed it is enough to replay the world
headless (`vlr sim -- --seed <seed> --minutes 35`) and compare your run with the policy's.
