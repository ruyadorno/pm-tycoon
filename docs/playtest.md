# Playtest 3: the brief and `playtest.json` v3

## The brief

Play **thirty to forty minutes, cold**, on the iPhone 16: a new game, no seed typed, no `?config=`, no help beyond what
the game shows. **Answer the cards as you like**: there is no right answer the build is waiting for. The run to watch
is level 1 → level 3 (the Docks and the ship) and, if it goes well, level 5 and the Golden Release. The policy timeline
(the 10-seed sim gate's medians with the card deck on, docs/gate/latest.md) puts L2 at ~10.7 min, L3 at ~16.5, L4 at
~20.6 and L5 at ~25.3. That is game time: the pauses on cards come on top, and a cold human is expected later. The
Golden Release is offered at L5 (60 deliveries in 5 days, no malware delivered, 85 % on time, down from 90 % in build
2). It is the stretch: the policy misses most offers on the 5-day clock and wins at 47.6–56.6 min on seeds 1–3
(docs/decisions.md, M14).

Build 3 adds the **cards** (README "Cards"): the first opens at 3:20 of game time (Day 4), then one every 60–100 s of
game time at L1, a little less often at higher levels. **Every card pauses the game** until you choose (no timer, no
Later) and prints each option's effects under it; a red option is the dangerous one, and a greyed one says what you
cannot afford. Job offers are `JOB BOARD` cards with **Accept** / **Decline**. The effects chip in the top bar (and
**Effects** in the pause menu) lists what is running. The Golden Release keeps its gold card, which does not pause the
game, and its **Later**. `playtest.json` v3 records the cards: every choice with its think time, the stories, and a
timeline of beats.

At **30:00 of game time** (and never before 25 real minutes on screen, so at 2× it waits for the wall clock) a ticker
line and a toast offer the run summary. Open it then if you are done, or keep playing and open it whenever you stop:
tap ❚❚ to pause, ❚❚ again for the pause menu → **End run — show summary**. The card shows the run's numbers and the
**fun bar 3** below; **Download playtest.json** saves the profile and **Copy summary** copies the card as text. Send
either with your notes.

The card's rows new in build 3:

- `Cards 18 · 16 decisions · 2 news`: the deck cards you answered, by kind (jobs stay on the `Quests` row). Nothing
  "slides": cards never expire.
- `Stories 2 · longest 4 cards`: chains finished (two-card pairs included) and the longest one you played through.
- `Longest quiet 48 s`: the longest stretch with nothing to do between 3:00 and level 2 (fun bar item 2's number;
  ` so far` until the window closes).
- `Lives lost 3 · 2 by cards`, with a sub row per lost life: a card's loss reads `chose: <the option>` under the card's
  title.

## Where to find it

`playtest.json` is the profile of one run: what happened, when, and how the phone coped. The recorder is
`src/metrics/playtest.ts` (plan.md §12.9, plan-build2 §8.7 task 1, plan-build3 §8.6 task 1, B3 §6), and the fun bar
lives in `src/metrics/funBar.ts`; `tests/metrics/playtest-schema.test.ts` pins every field and type, and
`tests/playtest/seed-7.json` is a real example written by `vlr shots` from a 35-minute automated run (seed 7, the
competent policy: the first 6 minutes in real time at 4×, then fast-forwarded, so its `elapsedS` and `fps` cover only
the real-time part). **The policy decides each card in the step that opens it**, so every `thinkS` in that file is 0,
`thinkMedianS` is 0 and fun bar item 2 reads `think 0 s`. That is the policy, not a broken clock: the schema test times
cards without a policy. Nothing is sent anywhere: the file stays on the device until you download or copy it.

## Units

- Every field ending in `S` is **sim seconds** (ticks ÷ 10), except `elapsedS`, `pausedS` and the think times, which
  are **wall-clock seconds**. At 1× speed one sim second is one real second; at 2× it is half of one.
- **v3** `events.choices[].thinkS` and `events.thinkMedianS` are wall seconds the card was **on screen**: not the time
  it waited behind a sheet or a paper (that counts in `pausedS`), not a hidden tab, not while the phone was sideways
  (the rotate card covers it), and a frame gap counts at most 1 s.
- **v3** `beats[].atS` and `events.choices[].atS` are **sim** seconds. A choice's `atS` is also when its card opened:
  the card holds the sim.
- One **in-game minute** = one **in-game day** = 60 sim seconds (spec §2): payday, satisfaction drift, the daily ticker
  line and the applicant pools' refresh happen on that boundary. A thirty-minute session at 1× is about thirty days.
- `null` means "not reached" (no truck bought yet, level 3 not reached, no Victory, …), never zero.
- Money is in game dollars, integers.

## The fun bar 3 (plan-build3 §9)

The card shows seven checks from the same object: ✓ pass, ✗ fail, `?` **you judge** (the data part holds, the rest is
how it felt — only you can say), `–` not yet (the window is still open).

1. **Decisions felt like mine** — at least 3 decision cards answered with a non-default option (`events.choices[]` with
   `kind: 'decision'` and `default: false`). Reads `?` with the count (`4 not default · you judge`): the real question is
   whether you can name one choice you regret or are proud of. Job and news cards never count (a job's Accept is not
   its default, so three accepted jobs would otherwise pass). ✗ below 3; `–` before the first decision card.
2. **Never idle on the ramp** — the window runs from 3:00 to the level-2 tick (`level2AtS`); before level 2 it ends at
   11:00 or the current time, whichever is earlier, and reads `–` until 11:00. The longest gap between `beats` inside
   it, edges included (3:00 to the first beat, the last beat to the window's end), must be ≤ 90 s (`gap 1:12 · think 9
   s`). ✗ over 90 s. With the gap in time, the soft half is the median think time: under 15 s (or no timed card) reads
   ✓; 15 s or more reads `?` — did the cards feel like interruptions? It never turns the check into ✗.
3. **L2 by 10:30, L3 by 16:30** — `level2AtS ≤ 630` and `level3AtS ≤ 990` (`L2 10:42 · L3 16:30`). ✗ when either came
   late, or is still missing past its time; ✓ when both made it; `–` while one is still open.
4. **A story (3+ cards)** — at least one story finished whose last card was its third or later (`choices[].chain.end`
   with `depth ≥ 3`; a two-card pair does not count, though `events.chainsFinished` and the `Stories` row include
   pairs). Reads `?` (`1 of 3+ · you judge`): did it read as one story? `–` while a story is still under way
   (`events.chainsRunning`), ✗ with only pairs (`pairs only`) or none (`none finished`).
5. **Kept playing past 25:00** — `elapsedS > 1500` at the moment you first opened the summary card (**real** minutes on
   screen, paused time included, not the game clock: at 2× the game clock runs ahead; the recorder keeps that moment,
   so a peek at 20:00 counts against it). The offer never comes before 25 real minutes, so opening it when offered
   passes.
6. **Every lost life explained** — every `livesLost[]` entry has a `counter`, a `sorter`, `clearedByPlayer: true`, or
   `cause: 'event'` with the `option` you chose (an overseas loss, `hub` set, also says "the ship has sailed" / "the
   plane has left" on its card). ✓ with none lost or all explained (`all 2 explained`), ✗ with the count of unexplained
   ones. A data check since build 3 (build 2 read `?`).
7. **(stretch) The Golden Release by 36:00** — `victoryAtS ≤ 2160`; `–` until 36:00, then ✗ (`none by 36:00`). The
   policy wins it at 47.6–56.6 minutes on seeds 1–3, so a ✗ here is not a failure of the build, just the Golden
   Release's 5-day clock.

The sim gate is the agent's proxy for items 2, 3 and 6 (docs/gate/latest.md, seeds 1–10 × 30 min with the card deck
on):

- **Item 2**: at least 3 decision cards between minute 3 and level 2 on every seed (median 4), and the Hold Bay at 8.2
  items per 10 minutes at level 1. The sorter's Hold Bay beats need no tap, but the sorter comes at level 2, when the
  window has closed.

## Reading a profile

- **The choice you regret (or are proud of)**: `events.choices` with `default: false`. `title` and `label` say which card
  and what you picked, and `atS` when (sim seconds; ÷ 60 for the game clock in minutes).
- **Your stories**: the `events.choices` whose `chain.end` is true. `chain.title` names the story and `chain.depth` how
  many cards it ran; `events.chainsRunning` stories were still under way at the end (started − finished can be larger:
  a follow-up whose card no longer applies is dropped and ends its story unfinished).
- **The quiet stretch**: take the `beats` between 180 and `level2AtS` (or 660 before level 2) and find the biggest gap
  (the `Longest quiet` row shows it). A long run of `hold` beats with no card means the Hold Bay kept you busy while the
  deck stayed quiet; a gap with no beats at all is idle time.
- **The cards' pace**: `thinkS` per choice (a long one on a decision card is a real deliberation; `thinkMedianS` leaves
  news out). `events.offered − events.decided` is a card still open when the profile was written, or a job card the
  Golden Release withdrew.
- **Pacing**: compare `level2AtS` … `level5AtS`, `victoryAtS` and `quests.firstCompletedS` with the gate medians above. A
  late level with a flat `moneyPerMin` means the fleet stayed small: check `vehiclesBought`, `docksBought` and
  `overseas.cleared` (overseas parcels pay ×1.5 from L3). Card cash is not in `moneyPerMin` and never moves the level
  bar.
- **People**: `hires` shows who you took and what you paid (`cheapest` false = you paid for pips); `fired`, `adsPosted`
  and `payroll.short` / `walkouts` show whether the crew felt manageable. `sorter.wrong` vs `sorter.decisions` is how much
  you could trust the sorter; `sorter.clearedMalware` is how often its `looks clean` tag was wrong.
- **Quests**: `quests.byTemplate` shows which jobs you took and finished; many `declined` means the job cards did not
  appeal, many `failed` that the deadlines felt unfair.
- **Lost lives**: `livesLost[].cause` splits malware from cards; a card's entry names the card (`label`) and your
  `option`.
- **The Hold Bay**: `holdBayEvents` vs `malware.tossedClean` and `malware.cleared` shows how you treated flagged parcels.
- **Phone performance**: `fps.p50 < 50` or `fps.p5 < 30` triggers the cut order of plan.md §12.8 (`src/render/quality.ts`).
  `device.zoom` should be 1 on the iPhone 16 (393 CSS px).
- **Not a cold run**: `buildStamp` ending in `cfg:spicy` / `cfg:tycoon`, or a `seed` you typed, means a debug session.
  A profile that started late (a Continue across builds, `?fastForward=`) has no choices, beats or stories from before
  it started, so items 1, 2 and 4 can read low while the `events` counters stay right.

Send the file (or the copied summary) with your notes; together with the seed it is enough to replay the world
headless (`vlr sim -- --seed <seed> --minutes 35`) and compare your run with the policy's (the policy will choose
differently on the cards, so the timelines part at the first card).
